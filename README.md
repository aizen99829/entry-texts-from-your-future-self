One line from the person who knows you best: you, ten years from now.

## The entry

> Hey, it's you from 2036. Stop Googling your symptoms at 3AM — it's the chair, not a disease. Also, buy the dip in 2027. You're welcome.

A warning, a diagnosis, and a tip — all from someone who has already
lived through the consequences of ignoring them.

## How it works

`main.py` writes exactly one line to `/output/entry.txt`. The line is
validated before writing: a single line, under 200 characters.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Why it works

The best text from your future self is specific enough to be creepy
and mundane enough to be believable. 3AM symptom-Googling is a habit
everyone recognises; the chair is the punchline nobody expects; and
the 2027 tip is the one thing only a time traveller could know.
